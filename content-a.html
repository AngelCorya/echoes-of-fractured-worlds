01 / CORE PROMISE

Nine runes. Fractured dimensions. One connected canon.

Echoes of Fractured Worlds is a cinematic dark-fantasy and science-fiction universe built to live across a novel, original music and a story-driven video game.

Mark is pulled from an ordinary life into a conflict spanning dimensions. A primordial talisman, nine runes and the worlds behind them force him to confront a question greater than power: what gives anyone the right to decide the fate of another world?
